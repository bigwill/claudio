What I recommend on the three layout questions

  1. Strips down, as lanes. Every grid shares one x axis, so the playhead is a single column running through all four strips, which shows "one clock" on screen. A 4-bar loop is 64 steps: about 12px per step as lanes, 4px across (switch the toggle and look at S4). This differs from the ASCII sketch in the plan.
  2. Design iterations in the strip, as a compact rail: distance bars, the newest distance, the latest rationale on one line, Cancel. The side panel gives Opus's reasoning more space, but it covers the other strips during soundcheck, when you are also picking bass and your own sound. Proposed fallback: clicking the rail opens the side panel for that one design.
  3. Chat in the right column. Threads need height, lanes need width, and a bottom chat squeezes both. When chat is at the bottom, the reply you are waiting for sits further from the strip it changes.

Additions the mockup makes beyond the plan, for you to accept or strike: the staged pattern is drawn as outlined cells on the grid (not only as the rail pip); in soundcheck, the top bar shows which design is blocking Space; the playhead also lights the current chord in the top bar; the you lane lights the keys you are holding. None of these needs a schema change.

Library drawer B on a strip

  • Opens on the right, over chat, filtered to the strip: bass shows bass sounds, keys shows keys sounds, and you shows every pitched sound. Drums never open it (kit only; B shows drums use the kit).
  • Three groups, newest first: Starters, Designed, Tweaks.
  • Row: name, role hue, provenance on a second line: designed from electric_piano.wav · this jam, described: "round rubbery sub…" · jam 4KQ2, tweak of Glass EP by keys, starter. The current sound is marked.
  • Keys: ↑↓ move, moving auditions it on the keyboard (your keys play the highlighted sound), Enter picks (lands at the next loop line in the jam, instantly in soundcheck), Esc closes. The dock's mode reads PICKER.
  • Picking on a thinking musician cancels its turn first; a toast says so.
  • The last row is always Design a new one: drop a WAV or describe it, which is mouse-only.
