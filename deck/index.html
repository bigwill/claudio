The workIngredients

A band brings instruments. You have to build yours.

In electronic music the palette isn't bought, it's authored. Before a note gets written, somebody decides what the thing even sounds like.

  • too brightthe bell you wanted glassy, not glaring
  • no thumbthe bass has body but no attack
  • won't hitthe kick is loud and still doesn't land

Hundreds of these a session. Every one of them yours.

The engineFM, in one breath

Vibrato, pushed past hearing, becomes timbre.

Past roughly 20 Hz the ear stops following the wobble and it collapses into a new tone. Huge range, almost no computation.

The gapIntention → parameters

The panel isn't the problem. The translation is.

There's no brightness parameter in an FM engine. Where a synth offers you one, a human hand-built it — a macro over modulation levels, tuned for that patch.

The costTwo modes, one session

Patching is not playing.

Playing is fast, physical, in time. Patching is slow, analytical, out of time. Nobody is in both at once — and the session is mostly the second one.
